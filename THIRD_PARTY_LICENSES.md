Procedurally generated for each reel by this project's own code; no third-party recordings.
Optional CC0 tracks would be listed in `assets/music/licences.json` (none are included).

## Art
Story art in `pages/simplestorymode/queue/` is supplied by the owner (made with an AI image tool;
posts using it carry Instagram's AI label and a caption line). Placeholder backgrounds in
